NOVATECH CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

COMPANY INFORMATION

NovaTech is a fictional technology company that provides software products and digital services to small businesses and individuals.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time (PKT).

Customers can contact support through support@novatech.example.

PRODUCTS

NovaDesk is a cloud-based productivity platform designed for small businesses.

NovaDesk includes task management, team collaboration, file sharing, and project tracking.

PRICING

NovaDesk Basic costs $10 per user per month.

NovaDesk Professional costs $25 per user per month.

NovaDesk Enterprise pricing is customized based on business requirements.

REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a refund within 14 days of their initial purchase.

Refund requests must include the customer's account email and order information.

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5 to 10 business days.

CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers can cancel their subscription at any time.

After cancellation, the customer can continue using the service until the end of the current billing period.

DATA SECURITY

NovaTech uses encryption and access controls to protect customer information.

Customers should never share their passwords or authentication codes with support staff.

SHIPPING

Physical products are not currently offered by NovaTech, so there are no shipping charges.

CONTACT
